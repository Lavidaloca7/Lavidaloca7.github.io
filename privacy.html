Privacy Policy

Shorts Upload · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who operates this app

Shorts Upload is operated by the creator of the YouTube channel WantedMoments for their own personal use. Contact: wantedmoments6@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives an OAuth access token for the owner's own YouTube account. With it, the app:

  • uploads video files that the owner created, together with the title, description, tags and privacy setting the owner chose;
  • reads the owner's own channel name and list of uploaded videos (to show which clips are already online and avoid duplicates).

The app does not access data of any other person, channel or Google account.

How data is stored

The OAuth token and a local upload log are stored only on the owner's own computer. No server, database or cloud storage operated by this app exists. Nothing is stored or processed outside the owner's computer, except by Google itself when videos are uploaded to YouTube.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Google API Services

Shorts Upload uses YouTube API Services. By using this app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also ends the app's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: wantedmoments6@gmail.com.
